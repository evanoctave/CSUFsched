# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CSUF students planning an upcoming semester. They need to compare real course sections, build a conflict-free weekly schedule, and see how each choice fits their recommended degree pathway.

## Product Purpose

CSUFsched combines the university's published course schedule, program roadmaps, and approved General Education (GE) data in one planning workspace. It helps students make informed schedules, but never replaces their Titan Degree Audit (TDA) or academic adviser.

## Brand Personality

Titan-proud, clear, and grounded. The interface should feel like a dependable campus planning tool with enough warmth and energy to make course planning feel approachable.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, loud athletics branding, invented university marks, decorative gradients, and any pathway or GE recommendation that is not backed by an official CSUF source.

## Design Principles

1. Show degree context beside scheduling decisions.
2. Use source-backed recommendations and identify their catalog year.
3. Keep planning controls direct, compact, and easy to recover from.
4. Let Titan Blue carry structure and Titan Orange signal action, never body text.
5. Make uncertainty visible: a source gap is safer than a fabricated recommendation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Support full keyboard operation, visible focus states, reduced motion, responsive layouts, and non-color status labels. Roadmaps must always retain a titled official-source link for assistive technology and PDF-reader access.
